How AF works

1. Diagnose the system

We identify the gaps between strategy, execution, data, and decisions.

2. Prioritize by business impact

We rank the work by expected impact, confidence, effort, and measurement readiness.

3. Build the operating layer

We create the pages, campaigns, workflows, QA loops, dashboards, and robots needed to make the strategy repeatable. Deterministic systems handle accuracy; agentic swarms handle repeatable execution; humans review the work that carries risk.

4. Use evidence to move budget

We turn reporting into decisions: fund, cut, repair, test, or scale.
