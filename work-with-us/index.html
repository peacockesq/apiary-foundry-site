1. Growth System Audit

A full diagnostic of the current marketing system.

Best for

Companies with active marketing work, messy attribution, unclear channel performance, or suspicion that too much effort is turning into too little impact.

  • positioning and offer clarity
  • paid media structure
  • landing pages and conversion paths
  • SEO/content system
  • CRM and lifecycle workflows
  • tracking and attribution
  • dashboards and reporting
  • automation opportunities

Output

A prioritized growth system map:

  • what is working

  • what to repair
  • what to build next
  • what deserves funding

How AF works

1. Diagnose the system

We identify the gaps between strategy, execution, data, and decisions.

2. Prioritize by business impact

We rank the work by expected impact, confidence, effort, and measurement readiness.

3. Build the operating layer

We create the pages, campaigns, workflows, QA loops, dashboards, and robots needed to make the strategy repeatable.

4. Use evidence to move budget

We turn reporting into decisions: fund, cut, repair, test, or scale.
