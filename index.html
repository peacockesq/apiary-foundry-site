01

What gets measured gets funded.

Marketing teams rarely lose because they lack ideas. They lose because the work turns into random acts of marketing:

  • campaigns launched before tracking is clean
  • landing pages built before the offer is clear
  • content published without search intent or distribution
  • leads routed without speed or accountability
  • dashboards patched together after decisions are already made
  • meetings full of opinions because the data cannot settle the argument

Apiary Foundry exists to end that cycle.

We make strategy operational, execution measurable, and automation accountable. Then we use the evidence to decide where the next dollar should go.

02

The Apiary operating model

Clean data before clever marketing

Your CRM, forms, lead sources, conversion values, and follow-up data need to be trustworthy before anyone should believe the dashboard. Apiary builds the scoreboard first so budget decisions are based on evidence, not vibes.

AI agents for the repeatable work

Agents help with audits, reporting, QA, campaign checks, content operations, workflow monitoring, and follow-up loops. They can pull messy inputs together and surface issues faster than a small team can do manually.

Human strategy for the judgment calls

The machines move fast. A human operator decides what is worth doing, what is risky, what the buyer will understand, and where the budget should go.

03

What AF builds

Enterprise-grade tracking without enterprise bloat

Small businesses should not need a Fortune 500 software budget to know which marketing work deserves money. Apiary can work with your existing tools, but we can also bring practical open-source and managed infrastructure into the engagement so you are not paying five vendors just to find out where leads came from.

Strategy that survives contact with execution

Apiary starts with the economics: audience, offer, funnel, sales process, margin, expected conversion rates, and the measurement plan. A marketing idea is only useful when the team can execute it and prove whether it worked.

Robots that protect the work

AI agents and automations handle repeatable pieces: audits, briefs, QA checks, reporting, routing, content operations, campaign diagnostics, and follow-up loops. Human judgment sets the strategy. Review systems keep the robots honest.

Know where the money went

Apiary treats click IDs, conversion values, CRM stages, lead sources, and offline conversion feedback as growth infrastructure. If the data disappears between the ad click and the closed deal, the business is forced back into guesswork.

Build work that answers budget questions

Every landing page, content asset, paid campaign, email sequence, and dashboard should help answer a funding question. The work has to move budget, pipeline, revenue, or learning.

04

The Five Hives

Most marketing problems are not isolated. Bad tracking ruins paid media. Weak offers hurt landing pages. Slow follow-up wastes good leads. The Five Hives organize the whole growth system so the pieces actually work together.

1. Acquisition Hive

Paid media and demand capture systems built around funnel economics, tracking integrity, offer testing, and budget decisions.

2. Content & Search Hive

SEO and content systems built around search intent, topical authority, expert voice, refresh loops, and distribution.

3. Conversion Hive

Landing pages, offers, forms, proof, and CRO systems that turn expensive traffic into measurable action.

4. Lifecycle Hive

CRM, nurture, routing, sales handoff, reactivation, and follow-up systems that keep leads from dying after the first form fill.

5. Measurement Hive

Attribution, warehouse logic, dashboard discipline, click ID retention, conversion values, and offline feedback loops.
