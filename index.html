02

The Apiary triumvirate

Deterministic systems for repeatable work that has to be right

Tracking, attribution, CRM fields, dashboards, routing rules, and conversion feedback loops need accuracy before they need creativity. AF builds these as deterministic systems first so the business is not asking AI to invent the scoreboard.

AI agents for the random, novel, and creative work

Agents are useful when the work changes every time: mining thousands of search terms for winners and negatives, QAing pages, drafting bespoke outreach, adapting nurture emails to company size and industry, and spotting patterns humans would miss. AF uses them for leverage, then routes the work through secondary review and human approval when the stakes demand it.

Human strategy from someone who understands law, business, and growth

Willie brings legal judgment, Chicago Booth MBA training, 15+ years of operating experience, paid media discipline, and funnel economics to the big-picture calls. The machine accelerates the work; human strategy decides what is worth doing.

03

What AF builds

Enterprise-grade tracking without enterprise bloat

Small businesses should not need a Fortune 500 software budget to know which marketing work deserves money. AF uses open-source tools, focused automation, clean CRM architecture, and practical dashboards to replace needless tool sprawl where cheaper infrastructure can do the job.

Strategy that survives contact with execution

AF starts with the economics: audience, offer, funnel, sales process, margin, expected conversion rates, and the measurement plan. A marketing idea is only useful when the team can execute it and prove whether it worked.

Robots that protect the work

AI agents and automations handle the repeatable pieces: audits, briefs, QA checks, reporting, routing, content operations, campaign diagnostics, and follow-up loops. Human judgment sets the strategy. Secondary review systems keep the robots honest.

Data architecture that earns trust

AF treats click IDs, conversion values, CRM stages, lead sources, and offline conversion feedback as growth infrastructure. If the data disappears between the ad click and the closed deal, the business is forced back into guesswork.

Pages and campaigns built for decisions

Every landing page, content asset, paid campaign, email sequence, and dashboard should answer one question: does this work deserve more budget? The output has to move pipeline, revenue, or learning.

1. Acquisition Hive

Paid media and demand capture systems built around funnel economics, tracking integrity, offer testing, and budget decisions.

2. Content & Search Hive

SEO and content systems built around search intent, topical authority, expert voice, refresh loops, and distribution.

3. Conversion Hive

Landing pages, offers, forms, proof, and CRO systems that turn expensive traffic into measurable action.

4. Lifecycle Hive

CRM, nurture, routing, sales handoff, reactivation, and follow-up systems that keep leads from dying after the first form fill.

5. Measurement Hive

Attribution, warehouse logic, dashboard discipline, click ID retention, conversion values, and offline feedback loops.
