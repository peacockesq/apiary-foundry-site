The point

Operating principle

  • Fix the measurement layer so growth decisions are based on business reality, not platform fiction. Keep claims, consent, and follow-up risk visible while the system scales.

Best for

Teams with mismatched CRM, ad platform, and revenue reporting; companies that need offline conversion tracking; and regulated categories with consent, claims, or communication-risk concerns.
