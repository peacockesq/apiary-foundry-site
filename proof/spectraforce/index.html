01

The company

Spectraforce is a national staffing agency placing clinical research, IT, and engineering professionals with pharmaceutical and biotech companies. The recruiting model depends on a steady flow of qualified candidates matched to active job requisitions.

The marketing challenge was scale: dozens of job types, multiple geographic markets, and a candidate journey that starts with awareness, moves through application, and ends with placement. A traditional agency would have scoped this as a six-month build. The leadership needed it faster.

02

The problem

Spectraforce had a small marketing team, a tight timeline, and an ambitious scope. The existing infrastructure was minimal: some paid campaigns, a basic website, and a CRM that was not configured for the complexity of the recruiting funnel.

The specific requirements:

  • Landing pages for every major job category and geographic market (50+ pages)
  • Email nurture sequences for candidates at different stages of interest (150+ emails)
  • HubSpot integration for lead capture, scoring, and routing
  • N8N-based lead routing to move form submissions to recruiter queues automatically
  • Self-booking calendar system so candidates could schedule consultations without email tag
  • Google Ads conversion tracking that actually reflected the candidate-to-placement funnel

A traditional agency would have staffed this with a project manager, two designers, a developer, a copywriter, and an automation specialist. Timeline: six months. Budget: significant.

03

The build: AI agents at production speed

Willie had been experimenting with agentic AI tools. Spectraforce was the first real deployment. The goal was to use AI for deterministic, repetitive, and parallelizable build work so the human operator could focus on strategy, review, and integration.

What the AI built:

  • 50+ landing pages in days, not weeks — each with job-specific messaging, location context, and conversion tracking
  • 150+ nurture emails across multiple sequences — candidate onboarding, job alert triggers, re-engagement, and placement follow-up
  • HubSpot integration — forms, lead scoring rules, pipeline stages, and automated routing logic
  • N8N workflow automation — connecting form submissions to recruiter queues with conditional logic based on role, location, and availability
  • Self-booking calendars — integrated into the landing pages so candidates could schedule without the email delay that kills warm leads
  • Custom tracking pipeline — rebuilt Google Ads tracking that had been broken by prior developers, ensuring conversion events reflected actual candidate value

What the human did:

  • Architected the system and defined the build order
  • Reviewed every AI-generated output before it touched a production system
  • Fixed tracking scripts that human developers had broken
  • Handled integration with legacy systems the AI could not access
  • QA"d the full funnel end-to-end before launch

The two-week sprint was not a miracle. It was the result of treating AI as a production tool, not a novelty — and of having an operator who knew what the output should look like.

04

The parallel: the bank build

Years earlier, at a regional bank launching a VA loan division, Willie built something similar: a call center, lead piping, drip campaigns, and CRM workflows — from zero infrastructure to a functioning system. It took about six months and required coordination across twenty people.

The Spectraforce build was the same scope, compressed into two weeks. The difference was not better project management or more caffeine. It was AI-assisted production at a scale and speed that was not possible before.

That is why this case study matters for the future of Apiary Foundry"s client work. The agency does not sell "AI hype." It sells "the same system build, in a fraction of the time, at a fraction of the cost, with a human operator keeping the output accurate."

05

What happened next

The Spectraforce engagement ended when the parent company reorganized and shifted budget away from U.S.-based marketing staff. The AI-built infrastructure remained.

On departure, Willie delivered a detailed handoff document explaining how every system was configured: the HubSpot objects, the N8N workflows, the landing page structure, the tracking scripts, and the calendar integrations. The remaining team was told: if anything breaks, reach out. AF stands by its work even when the paycheck stops.

The Spectraforce case study is the proof point for AI-assisted marketing operations at scale. It shows what is possible when an experienced operator uses agentic tools for execution while maintaining human control over strategy, accuracy, and quality.
