01

Why the attorney background matters

Willie Peacock is admitted to practice in nine U.S. jurisdictions. His legal work has focused on QDROs — the court orders that divide retirement accounts during divorce. That means every case involves:

  • Federal statutes (ERISA)
  • Plan-specific rules for dozens of different retirement systems
  • Financial calculations that affect someone"s retirement security
  • Deadlines that, if missed, cost clients thousands of dollars
  • No room for guesswork or spin

That practice trained a specific instinct: check the data, respect the complexity, and build systems that do not let details slip through cracks. It is the same instinct Willie brings to marketing measurement, tracking architecture, and AI-assisted workflows.

The legal-tech work matters too. Willie has built automated document systems, structured intake workflows, and expert review loops. He knows that automation without accuracy is worse than no automation at all — because bad automation scales mistakes faster than humans can catch them.

02

How AF treats data and claims

Marketing agencies routinely make claims they cannot substantiate. Apiary Foundry does not operate that way.

Every number on the AF site is sourced from actual work. Every case study references a real engagement. Every proof point is tied to a system Willie built or operated. There is no invented case study, no rounded-up metric, no hypothetical "up to 300% ROI" language that sounds impressive and means nothing.

The same standard applies to client work:

  • Tracking is deterministic — click IDs, UTMs, and conversion values are preserved as system-of-record, not summarized after the fact
  • Attribution is auditable — the path from ad click to revenue event can be traced and verified
  • Dashboards are honest — they show what happened, not what the team wishes happened
  • Claims are defensible — if a landing page makes a promise, the proof is in the system, not the copywriter"s optimism
  • AI output is checked — agentic work runs through secondary review before it touches a client system

This is not legal advice, and AF does not provide legal services. But the legal training shapes how the agency approaches risk, accuracy, and accountability.

04

The QDRO discipline

QDRO work sounds obscure. It is. That is exactly why it shaped the AF approach.

A QDRO (Qualified Domestic Relations Order) divides retirement benefits in divorce. Every plan has different rules. Every calculation affects someone"s financial future. Most family law attorneys avoid the work because it is complex and unglamorous.

Willie built a practice around it by treating it as a systems problem:

  • Document automation that reduces errors without eliminating attorney judgment
  • Structured intake that captures the variables that matter for each plan type
  • Deadline tracking that prevents costly administrative delays
  • Flat-fee economics that reward efficiency, not billing-hour inflation

The same principles show up in AF marketing systems: structured workflows, deterministic accuracy, deadline awareness, and economics that reward the right behavior.
